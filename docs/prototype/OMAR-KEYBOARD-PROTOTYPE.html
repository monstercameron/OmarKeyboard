Omar key guide 0%

Use either thumbSuper + arrow focuses a window. Super + Space opens Menu. Super + Tab visits the next desk. Next Window cycles windows, with Shift to reverse.

Choose a deskThe five desk keys sit above typing digits 1 through 5. Last Desk returns to the previous one. Send & Stay and Move & Go use the five desk keys.

Take a picture or copy wordsScreenshot opens the capture tool. Grab Text extracts words from a screen region so you can paste them as text.

Take a window with youTap Move & Go, then a desk key. Your focused window moves there and you follow it.

Send a window aheadTap Send & Stay, then Desk 3. The window moves to desk 3 and you stay where you are. Esc or a second tap on the same action cancels. Modifier keys (Ctrl, Shift, Super), Caps/Fn, the dials and the roller do not cancel a pending transfer. Another key does.

Return to your editorLast Desk takes you back to the previous desk on this monitor.

Pick or swap a tileHold Focus or Swap while pressing an arrow. Releasing the control immediately restores ordinary arrows. A quick accidental tap has no effect.

Use the Fn legendsTap Caps for Caps Lock. Hold it for Fn. Fn does only what is printed in the Fn colour: Left/Right give Home/End, Up/Down give Page Up/Down, Delete gives Insert, and Next Window returns to the last window. Every other key types normally. A transfer expires after three seconds.

Right CtrlThe Ctrl key beside the arrows sends Right Control. Hold it with Left or Right for word navigation in editors. Dedicated desk and window keys generate their own shortcuts.

Adjust the dialsVolume presses mute speakers. Shift + Menu opens Apps. The right dial adjusts brightness, Fn + turn resizes the split, and pressing cycles layouts. Fn + roller delivers Page Up/Down to the focused application.

Pick the next windowTap Next Window to cycle forward or add Shift to reverse. Fn + Next Window returns to the last focused window on this desk with the optional Omar bindings. Super + K opens Omarchy’s live shortcut list.

Open and editMenu is beside Caps on the left edge. Delete is above Backspace. Clipboard opens history and Paste inserts the current clipboard item into the focused app.

Read the secondary legendsHold Super with W for Close, C for Copy, or V for Paste. The word is printed on the key.
