## What to order first

1. **Buy a small control sample for the hand-wired pilot.** One PICO30, two EN11 dial encoders, one Alps roller sample and one MR63-ZZ bearing cost **$13.28 + €4.50** before shipping. Fit them to a printed corner and roller fixture. Measure knob height with the push switch depressed, shaft engagement, roller drag and one output step per detent.
2. **Print the full layout before cutting the plate.** Check the two 100 mm scale bars, hold Focus while reaching each arrow, and test the recessed Lock. Print or cut one switch-and-stabilizer coupon before committing to a 406 mm plate. [Full-size template](../docs/prototype/OMAR-KEYBOARD-PROTOTYPE.html)
3. **Release the expensive parts after the assembled fit and service trial.** Confirm encoder and socket footprints, the 2 mm underside clearance and USB plug access. Get separate prices for the PCB lot, plate and keycaps, with freight and minimum quantities. Have the cap vendor confirm the unusual row/width combinations and tactile marks in writing.

Use the [hardware specification](specification.md) for the height stack and hole datums, and the [switch-coordinate CSV](layout/omar-switches.csv) for each cap width, row, matrix address and LED index.
